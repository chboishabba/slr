## Correct signed proposition semantics

For a ground-scoped predicate `P` (such as alleged defect):

| Fine-evidence coordinate | Coarse SSP sign | Synthesis instruction |
|---|---:|---|
| Positive support for precisely `P` only | +1 | Candidate applicant defeater test, if source-review predicate is stipulated |
| Negative support for precisely `¬P` only | −1 | Candidate respondent counter-defeater test, if source-review predicate is stipulated |
| Neither `P` nor `¬P` supplied | 0 | Seek originals; refine to lower decision tree |
| Both supports supplied | 0 | Preserve contradiction in fine fibre and reconcile; never explode into either finding |

**+ and − refer to two explicitly identified versions of the SAME
predicate.** A respondent's unrelated position, inverted orientation,
or geometry antipode is not automatically the logical negation of
an applicant predicate. DASHI explicitly types `negativeProposition
g slot = PNF.notF (baseProposition g slot)`, while importing the
pre-existing `ClaimIndexedEvidencePolarityExact` and
`BalancedTernaryOppositionEvidenceBridgeExact` separation of logical
negation from orientation reversal.

Original logic roots: **J. Michael Dunn (1976)**,
*Intuitive Semantics for First-Degree Entailments and 'Coupled Trees'*;
**Nuel D. Belnap (1977)**, *A Useful Four-Valued Logic*.
The inherited DASHI code is independently authored; the original
mathematical source and kernel proof owner are not conflated.
See https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-manyvalued/ .
The finite projection of these four information states to the three
trit signs loses information **by design**: both and neither map to
0. The new fine fibre retains the two support Booleans and original
reference labels, and Agda `noUniversalClassFromCoarse` shows why a
universal four-way decoder cannot be recovered from one trit.

## Existing receivers, no duplicate framework

1. **PNF proposition** — `SpacyDependencyToCandidateLogicalPNFExact`
   builds typed atoms and an explicit `notF` for the same predicate.
   The question is `(ground,slot,scope,source provenance)`, not an
   untyped English claim or free-floating sign.
2. **Claim fibre** — `DisagreementFourViewBoundary` and
   `ClaimIndexedEvidencePolarityExact` retain separate positive/
   negative evidence, both and neither, with same-scope alignment
   requirements before merging.
3. **Nine-position 369 chart** —
   `Base369Ternary27HypervoxelFabricGeometryExact` gives three
   full 27-point cubes `T^3×T^3×T^3=T^9`.
   A: applicable law, defect, materiality.
   B: claimed compliance, habitat concern, causal connection.
   C: reviewed original, same-project stage, independent mediation.
   All **nine** are claim-indexed signed predicates, not a mix
   of six Boolean premises and three derived outputs.
4. **Nested predicate tree** — `PredicateDecisionTree` is recursively
   typed in Agda. The SLR output exposes a concrete two-level
   three-branch tree (A/B/C and their two child predicates), retaining
   fine evidence on each node. More nesting needs additional source
   and scope work, not an automatic vote.
5. **Typed hyperfabric** — `TypedHyperfabricCore` retains a whole
   signed-ground fibre as a vertex and edge stalk. Same-ground
   incidence is identity; cross-project/scope evidence joins cannot
   arise from equal numeric signs. The constructed `GlobalSection`
   is a *hypothetical* compatibility section, not an actual reviewed
   source or authority.
6. **Hypercomplex receiver** — `HypercomplexRequestedFractranComponentExact`
   and `RequestedFibreInvolutionFoldExact` separate a fine vector,
   graph, hypercomplex or contextual FRACTRAN valuation from the
   coarse SSP trit. A coarse **zero is not necessarily a fine fixed
   point**; it can be an unobserved or collapsed contradictory fibre.
   No manufactured legal prime / Monster 3B representation was added.
7. **3-adic prefix** — `TriadicPAdicCylinderExact` keeps a chosen
   low-to-high sequence of signed decisions. Prefix refinement retains
   earlier digits, as checked by its original compatibility theorem.
   A finite list of source decisions is not an infinite inverse limit,
   not a physical 3-adic quantity and not a flattening of a branching
   tree. SLR exposes one selected finite prefix with
   `full_inverse_limit_established=false`.
8. **Trialectic carry** — `TrialecticThreeCellCarryDepthExact` supplies
   the conditional depth+1 cell promotion *only after an explicit
   higher face-gluing witness*. No missing legal or ecological evidence
   is generated by this conditional carry. The attached face remains
   underdetermined.
