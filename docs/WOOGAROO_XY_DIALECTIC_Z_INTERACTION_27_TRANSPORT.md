## Exact axes and nine XY encounters

- **X** is ONE dialectic's current **thesis, antithesis, synthesis** role.
- **Y** is ANOTHER dialectic's **thesis, antithesis, synthesis** role.
- **Z** is the stage/role of **the interaction between those two
  dialectical positions**: its own thesis, antithesis, synthesis.

At fixed Z, the 3 × 3 XY plane contains the nine *ordered* encounters:

| Y \ X | X thesis | X antithesis | X synthesis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Y thesis | (T,T,Z) | (A,T,Z) | (S,T,Z) |
| Y antithesis | (T,A,Z) | (A,A,Z) | (S,A,Z) |
| Y synthesis | (T,S,Z) | (A,S,Z) | (S,S,Z) |

Each XY encounter forms one vertical column of **three possible Z states**.
The cube has `3 × 3 × 3 = 27` cells. The coordinate trit
`{-1,0,+1}` is used here **only as a typed positional code** for
`{thesis,antithesis,synthesis}`. In this geometric role chart, negative
coordinate `−1` does NOT mean evidence for logical `¬P`. Sign support
`−P/0/+P` is a different typed carrier in a claim/evidence fibre.

For X thesis, Y antithesis the Z column is exactly:
`(thesis,antithesis,thesis)`,
`(thesis,antithesis,antithesis)`,
`(thesis,antithesis,synthesis)`.
Z can travel between the first and second, and then second and third,
*if an independently supplied interaction rule/path* allows it.
This optional progression does not prove automatic resolution,
monotonic social evolution, legal decision, or universal synthesis.
