## Three loci and irreducible face

- **A, applicant/conservation:** a *candidate* assertion of omission, breach
  or ecological defect, never equated with a proved legal defect.
- **B, respondent/proponent:** the strongest currently stated *hypothetical*
  statutory, procedural or ecological countercase, never an admission or
  proven ministerial consideration.
- **C, independent assessment:** verify the exact law, dated decision file,
  action-specific baseline, same-object stage and expert evidence, followed
  by competent legal/ecological review. Not a third litigating party.

A↔B, B↔C and C↔A are recorded distinctly. We cannot infer the
triadic face from agreeing three dyadic edges. Because no competent
same-source/scope review has been paid, the **attached relational
2-cell remains `Underdetermined`**, even in hypothetical worlds whose
Boolean flag `evidence_reviewed=true` was stipulated for logical testing.
This adopts the original DASHI attached-face non-factorability theorem,
not a new proof or a same-object Monster representation.
