The case's production **priority frontier** is deliberately unscored. Four urgent candidate grounds (approved-advice consideration, comment transmission, approval scope and local prestart) precede recovery-plan and habitat merits work; speculative influence remains last unless original decision-channel evidence changes it. Those legal grounds are separate from four **unpaid procedural prerequisites**: (1) named applicant, historic objects and authority; (2) applicant-specific written furnishing and ADJR deadline; (3) actual same-stage works/urgency and prestart compliance; and (4) the signed federal decision/conditions, s95B and s95C package. All four carry `document_paid=false` and `competent_decision_paid=false`, and no court clock is assumed from the public portal alone.

The production Rust case module exports `WOOGAROO_SOURCE_LOCATORS` (nine original-author and exact-referral locators), `WOOGAROO_GROUNDS` (seven explicitly unresolved legal hypotheses), `WOOGAROO_PRECONDITIONS` (four unpaid counsel/decision/works prerequisites), `woogaroo_priority_frontier` (an unscored acquisition queue), `validate_woogaroo_case_cut` (reject invented paid source/review or filing refs), `woogaroo_locator_matches_springview` (project identity only, not source-merits admission), `woogaroo_candidate_matter` and `validate_woogaroo_candidate_matter` over the **existing** `MatterControversyDraft` engine. Each source record has `None` for durable source revision, content SHA, exact span and reviewed case evidence. Legal-atom gates are `0` and have no positive/negative witnesses, nor Court-filing reference.

This is **production-target source code**, not a deployed, persisted, reviewed or executable-evidence-backed legal case. All final signed federal conditions, Secretary s95C, actual proponent comment transmission, source bytes and case witnesses remain unpaid.
