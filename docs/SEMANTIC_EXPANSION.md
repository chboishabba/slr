# Post-certification semantic expansion

The GWB v0.1 certification applies to the exact Rust head
`60777f637732f28fed46458a30853d35b88a8a09` and the direct/reference observation
surface exercised by that receipt.

The `sensiblaw-semantic-expansion` crate is deliberately post-certification and
experimental. It does not alter the certified direct core or publication path.

Its current contract mirrors the DASHI PNF algebra:

- richer semantic roles are candidate structure, not parser-proven world facts;
- scoped negation remains unresolved until scope evidence exists;
- modality and temporal anchors remain explicit residuals when unresolved;
- conditional markers produce candidate condition structure, not causal truth;
- ambiguous clausal modifiers retain an alternative fibre rather than selecting
  one interpretation;
- reference and qualifier attachments remain candidate-only until resolved;
- the crate exposes no generation publication API.

A future activation must earn new direct/reference parity and performance receipts
for this expanded observation surface. The GWB v0.1 receipt does not automatically
cover these new candidate structures.
